01 — About

Our Mission

The AI Capital Group exists to be a think-tank for one of the most consequential transitions in modern finance: the restructuring of entire industries by artificial intelligence.

We take a rigorous and visionary approach to evaluating AI’s capacity in reshaping competitive advantage capital allocation, and value creation across the economy.

Our excellence is embedded within our core values: curiosity, rigour, conviction, and innovation. Curiosity opens the questions, rigour tests the thesis, conviction acts on the answers, and innovation ensures we never mistake those answers for the end.

02 — Vanguard Fund

AIC Vanguard Fund

Our fund is built around a defined investment mandate, a rigorous bottom-up analytical process, an actively managed portfolio, and published research we stand behind. Our thesis is not stagnant; we reassess and recalibrate our mandate every quarter. In a market defined by relentless innovation and constant disruption, intellectual rigidity is a liability. By regularly revisiting our core analytical framework, we ensure our portfolio reflects the most current and defensible view of where AI is creating and destroying value.

Current Thesis

Q4 2026 Thesis

We deploy capital into publicly traded legacy software, running a concentrated book of high-conviction positions sized by conviction and downside risk. We screen for companies where the selloff appears sentiment-driven rather than fundamental. Each company is run through a two-pronged analysis, and all positions require a completed investment memo and a formal investment committee vote before capital is deployed.

Prong I — Defensibility

Does the company possess proprietary data, complex infrastructure, deep back-end engineering, or hard-to-replicate scale that insulates it from disruption?

Prong II — AI Leverage

Can the company actively harness AI to improve its products, reduce costs, or drive meaningful operational efficiency? Companies that pass both prongs form our shortlist.

Looking Forward

The fund is the foundation. AI Capital is designed to grow well beyond it — expanding our capabilities, deepening our coverage, and adding new dimensions to the group over time.
