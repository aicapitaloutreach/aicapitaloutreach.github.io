01 — About

The Thesis

When a major technological shift hits markets, the first wave of repricing is almost always indiscriminate. AI is no different.

Companies are being written off before anyone has seriously asked whether they are actually vulnerable. Others are priced for perfection with no margin for error. That disconnect between what the market believes and what the fundamentals say is the space we were built to occupy.

AI Capital is a student-led investment group at the University of St Andrews, anchored around our fund: a defined investment mandate, a rigorous bottom-up analytical process, an actively managed portfolio, and published research we stand behind.

02 — The Fund

AIC Fund

We deploy capital into publicly traded legacy software, running a concentrated book of high-conviction positions sized by conviction and downside risk. We screen for companies where the selloff appears sentiment-driven rather than fundamental. Each company is run through a two-pronged analysis, and all positions require a completed investment memo and a formal investment committee vote before capital is deployed.

Prong I — Defensibility

Does the company possess proprietary data, complex infrastructure, deep back-end engineering, or hard-to-replicate scale that insulates it from disruption?

Prong II — AI Leverage

Can the company actively harness AI to improve its products, reduce costs, or drive meaningful operational efficiency? Companies that pass both prongs form our shortlist.

Looking Forward

The fund is the foundation. AI Capital is designed to grow well beyond it — expanding our capabilities, deepening our coverage, and adding new dimensions to the group over time.
